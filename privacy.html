Just Spades: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Just Spades is a card game made by Peter Okaru. This policy covers the Just Spades app on Android (package com.karu.justspades) and iOS.

The short version

You play offline against three computer players. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools, and no in-app purchases. The game itself never connects to the internet. The only time the app sends anything is when you choose to send feedback with the optional Feedback button (see below).

What the app collects

Nothing, unless you choose to send feedback. The app doesn't send any information about you, your device, or how you play in the background.

Optional tester feedback

Test versions of the app (and possibly later versions) show a small Feedback button. Nothing is sent unless you type a message and tap Send. When you do, the app sends the following to me through Formspree, a form-handling service:

  • Your message (up to 1,000 characters).
  • The name or email you enter in the optional "Your name or email" field, if any. If it looks like an email address, it's also used as the reply-to address so I can answer you.
  • App and device information: the app version and build number, the platform (Android or iOS), the operating system version, and the device model (for example "Google Pixel 8"; on iPhone this may be "unknown").
  • Where you sent it from: the home screen or the game table.
  • If you send it from the game table, a short game summary: the hand number, the phase of the hand, the trick number, both teams' scores and bags, the bids, the tricks taken, whether spades are broken, and the game's rule settings. It doesn't include your cards or anything about you.

Like any website you connect to, Formspree also receives your device's IP address as part of the connection. Formspree processes the submission under its own privacy policy: https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

I use feedback only to fix problems and improve the game. I don't sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising. To have your feedback deleted, email npobusiness@gmail.com and tell me roughly when you sent it (and the name or email you entered, if any).

What stays on your device

The game saves a few small settings on your phone so they're still there next time:

  • Your five saved chat responses, if you edit them.
  • Whether bot chat is muted.
  • Your sound preferences (master sound toggle and volume).
  • Whether hidden Developer Mode is turned on (and any developer sound-timing offsets). It's a testing panel, off by default.

These settings never leave your device. The chat itself is between you and the computer players. It's kept in memory only and cleared when you start a new game. A game in progress isn't saved.

To delete these settings, uninstall the app. On Android you can also clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Permissions

Just Spades never asks for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

  • iOS: the app doesn't show any permission prompts.
  • Android: the app uses one standard install-time permission, internet access, used only to send feedback when you tap Send. Microphone, overlay, and storage permissions are blocked on purpose.

App stores and other services

You download and update the game through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple may collect standard install, update, and device data under their own privacy policies. If you've agreed to share diagnostics on your device, they may also give me anonymous crash reports and download numbers through their developer consoles. I don't get anything that identifies you.

The app is built with Expo's EAS build service. The installed app doesn't send anything to Expo.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Expo Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy
  • Formspree Privacy Policy: https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

Children

Just Spades is meant for players aged 13 and up. It isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent feedback that includes personal information, email npobusiness@gmail.com and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

Online multiplayer may come in a future version. Before any version that sends more data over the internet is released, I'll update this policy, change the effective date above, and update the privacy details in the app store listings.

Contact
