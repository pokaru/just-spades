Just Spades: Support

Just Spades is a classic game of Spades for your phone. You play with a computer partner against two computer opponents. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

Need help? Email npobusiness@gmail.com. Testers can also use the Feedback button in the app (see below).

How to play

The basics. Four players play in two teams. Your partner sits across from you. Each player gets 13 cards. Everyone bids how many tricks (books) they think they'll take, then you play 13 tricks.

  • You must follow the suit that was led if you can.
  • Spades are always trump. The highest spade wins the trick. If no spade was played, the highest card of the suit that was led wins.
  • You can't lead a spade until spades have been "broken" (someone played a spade on another suit's trick), unless you only have spades left.

Jokers (optional). With jokers on, the Big Joker is the highest card and the Little Joker is next, followed by A♠, K♠, and the rest. The 2♦ and 2♥ are removed so there are still 52 cards. Classic games use jokers. In Custom games they're off by default.

Bidding.

  • Bid any number of tricks, or Nil (you promise to take none).
  • Your team's combined bid can't be more than 13.
  • Nil isn't allowed while you hold the highest trump: the Big Joker, or the A♠ in games without jokers.
  • Blind Nil: before you see your cards, the game asks "Blind Nil?" Tap Yes to bid blind nil, or No to see your cards and bid normally. Blind nil is always allowed when it's turned on.
  • Board (Custom option): your team must bid at least 4 tricks between you.

Scoring (defaults).

What happened Points
Team makes its bid 10 × bid, plus 1 per extra trick
Team misses its bid −10 × bid
Nil made / missed +100 / −100
Blind nil made / missed +200 / −200
Every 10 bags (extra tricks) −100

Tricks taken by a nil bidder still count toward the partner's bid. The game ends when a team reaches 150 (they win) or drops to −250 (they lose). You can change both limits.

Turn timer. You have 30 seconds to bid. If time runs out, the game bids 1 for you (or the nearest legal bid). The play timer is also 30 seconds; when it runs out, the game plays a legal card for you.

Game options

  • Quick Game: start right away with Classic settings.
  • Classic Game: standard house rules with jokers, nil and blind nil. You can only change the minimum and maximum score.
  • Custom Game: choose jokers, Board, nil and blind nil (on or off, with your own reward and penalty), the Spades Broken animation, Bag Heat Colors, Card Back, and Table Style. Some options (Public/Private, Game Name, and First Hand Bidding) are there for future versions and don't change the game yet.

Sending feedback (testers)

Test versions of Just Spades have a small Feedback button: on the home screen at the bottom left, next to Quick Game, and during a game at the top right of the table. Tap it, describe what happened or what you'd like to see (up to 1,000 characters), optionally add your name or email so I can reply, and tap Send. You'll see "Thanks! Sent." when it's arrived. If you're offline, you'll see an error and can tap Retry.

Along with your message, the app sends the app version, your phone model and OS version, and (from the game table) a short summary of the current game, such as the scores and rules, to help me reproduce problems. Your cards and contacts are never sent. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can always email npobusiness@gmail.com instead.

FAQ

How do I control sound effects or chat mute? Open Settings on the home screen to turn sound effects on or off and adjust the volume. The Mute button in the chat drawer mutes only the computer players' chat messages.

Who am I chatting with? Only the computer players. Pull out the chat drawer from the right edge of the screen to send a saved response or type a message. Bots react to the game. Chat never leaves your phone and is cleared when a new game starts.

How do saved responses work? You get five. Tap one to send it. Hold one to edit it, type your new text (up to 40 characters), and tap Save. If you save an empty response, it goes back to the default. Tap "Editing… tap to cancel" to stop without saving.

Will my settings survive a reinstall? No. Saved responses and the chat mute setting live only on your phone. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) resets them.

Is my game saved if I close the app? No. A game in progress isn't saved when the app is fully closed.

I see a "Developer" panel. What is it? It's a hidden testing tool. Tap your avatar on the home screen 7 times quickly to turn it off (or back on).

Does the game collect my data? No, unless you choose to send feedback with the Feedback button. See the Privacy Policy.

What do Friends, Learn to Play, and Search do? They're coming in a future update. For now, tapping one shows a "Coming soon" message. Settings lets you control sound effects, and on Android also includes a Close App button.

Troubleshooting

  • The screen looks cut off or sideways: Just Spades only plays in landscape. Rotate your phone and check that rotation lock isn't holding it in portrait.
  • The game seems stuck: wait a moment, because the computer players take a short time to think. If nothing happens, close the app fully and reopen it, then start a new game.
  • The game played a card or bid for me: the turn timer ran out. See "Turn timer" above.
  • I can't bid Nil: you're holding the highest trump (the Big Joker, or the A♠ without jokers), or nil is turned off in this Custom game.
  • I can't lead a spade: spades haven't been broken yet.
  • Something else: update to the latest version from Google Play or the App Store, then restart your phone.
